M&A AND COMMERCIAL INVESTMENTS

M&A and Commercial Investments

← All practice areas

Overview

  1. 01

    Domestic and cross-border deals

    Our M&A and Commercial Investments Department advises on mergers, acquisitions, asset sales, leveraged buy-outs, private equity financing, convertible loans, venture lending and other debt financings.

  2. 02

    Every stage of the deal

    From initial negotiations, through legal due diligence, to regulatory filings, consents and approvals – such as Office of the Chief Scientist approval for transferring IP outside Israel.

  3. 03

    Structuring and ongoing advice

    We help clients negotiate and structure the transaction and related commercial agreements, and provide continuous legal advice.

  4. 04

    Corporate activity

    Share and asset purchases, statutory mergers, exchange and cash tender offers, spin-offs, recapitalizations, joint ventures, court-approved shareholder arrangements, corporate governance, and management and employee incentive programs.

  5. 05

    Recent matters

    Stock Purchase Agreements for a $3M sale of stock of an Israeli cloud data-storage provider; VC financing rounds for one of Israel’s leading VC funds; Seed and Series A investments in e-commerce and SCADA; ongoing support to an e-commerce widget developer.
