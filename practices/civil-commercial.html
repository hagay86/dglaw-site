Overview

  1. 01

    Full commercial services

    Dardik, Gross & Co. provides legal services in all aspects of commercial law to individuals and corporations, in Israel and abroad.

  2. 02

    From deal to dispute

    Advising on and strategizing transactions, negotiating on the client’s behalf, drafting contracts, establishing and managing joint ventures, handling government agencies and regulators, drafting legal opinions, and litigating at all instances – including alternative dispute resolution.

  3. 03

    One-stop shop

    Loyal to the firm’s one-stop-shop credo, the department covers all of our clients’ needs: ongoing legal advice, labor law, public tenders, litigation, real estate transactions, IP and more.

  4. 04

    Global reach

    We have unique expertise with clients whose business requires thorough work with parties abroad. Several of our lawyers work regularly with colleagues and clients in the Americas, the UK, the EU and the Far East.
