LABOR LAW

Labor Law

← All practice areas

Overview

  1. 01

    Your people, your business

    A company’s employees are among the most important aspects of its lifetime. We specialize in Labor & Employment Law, mainly from the employer’s perspective, for a holistic approach to your business.

  2. 02

    Personal commitment

    We combine knowledge, expertise and experience with a personal commitment to each client’s needs, representing mostly business and corporate employers.

  3. 03

    Hearings and litigation

    Managing hearing procedures, representing employers and employees before the Israeli Labor Court, and handling motions for injunctions and the prevention of wrongful dismissals.

  4. 04

    Agreements and advice

    Employment, severance and non-competition agreements; advice on severance pay and social benefits; legal opinions; re-organizations and M&A; labor unions law; pension and social security.

  5. 05

    Integrated with immigration

    Our employee-migration department works together with our international commercial department – one integrated system for international clients doing business in Israel.
