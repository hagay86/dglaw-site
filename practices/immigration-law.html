IMMIGRATION LAW

Immigration Department

25+ Years of Excellence in Immigration Law - Ranked as Elite Firm

← All practice areas

Overview

  1. 01

    A leading immigration practice

    Dardik Gross & Co. is one of the leading and most active firms in Israel in the field of immigration, with more than 25 years of experience.

  2. 02

    Work permits in every industry

    We secure work permits for experts, managers and professionals relocated to Israel by multinational, foreign and Israeli companies – in Energy (Oil & Gas, Solar and Power stations), High-Tech, Industry, Engineering, Transportation, Aviation, Banking, Telecommunications, Pharmaceuticals and more.

  3. 03

    Visas and relocation

    We obtain B-1 expert work visas and extended B-2 visitor visas for families, and plan mobility solutions: bringing experts to Israel on challenging schedules, relocation including house and vehicle rentals, driver licenses, and everyday administrative matters.

  4. 04

    A dedicated team

    Our immigration department has 8 immigration service providers, including experienced labor and immigration lawyers, headed by Adv. Yaacov (Kobi) Neeman. Our Managing Director, Adv. Dan Gross, is Co-Chair of the Israel Bar Association Committee of Foreign Employees.

  5. 05

    Recognized excellence

    The firm recently received an award for the Best Work Visas (B-1) Team in Israel. BDI-Coface has given it its highest recognition as an “Elite” firm for immigration and relocation services, from 2014 to 2026.

  6. 06

    Our services

    B-1 work permits for foreign experts · Short-term work visas (up to 90 days) · ETA-IL · B-2 tourist/visitor visas · Work permits under the Law of Return · Spousal/partner immigration · Immigration violations defense (civil & criminal).
