Overview

  1. 01

    Infrastructure and natural resources

    We advise private, public and governmental bodies, local and foreign, on establishing and maintaining infrastructure and on the exploitation of natural resources.

  2. 02

    National-level projects

    Natural gas, civil engineering projects of national importance, and cutting-edge projects in renewable energy, transportation, trains and water technology.

  3. 03

    Who we advise

    Local and foreign entrepreneurs, funders, service providers and technology suppliers working in energy and infrastructure.

  4. 04

    Types of deals

    Privatization, BOT projects, PPP transactions and financing transactions.

  5. 05

    Anticipating challenges

    Our extensive experience lets us foresee the challenges of projects at this scale – including regulation, licenses and permits, tenders, financing, construction and operation.
