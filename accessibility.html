Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Dardik Gross & Co. believes that everyone, including people with disabilities, should be able to use our website fully and independently. We have invested resources to make this website accessible, in line with the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998, and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Accessibility Adjustments for Service) Regulations, 5773-2013.

Accessibility level

The website has been adapted to meet Israeli Standard IS 5568 (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines), which is based on the international WCAG 2.0 guidelines at level AA. The site has been checked on current versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, on desktop and mobile.

What we have done

  • Every page declares its language (English or Hebrew), and the Hebrew version is laid out right to left.
  • A consistent structure of headings, navigation and page regions, so the site can be used with screen readers.
  • Full keyboard navigation, with a clearly visible focus indicator and a “Skip to main content” link at the top of every page.
  • Text alternatives (alt text) for images.
  • Labels for all form fields.
  • Videos that play automatically have no sound and can be paused with a pause button.
  • The site adapts to different screen sizes and to zooming up to 200%.
  • An accessibility menu, via the button at the bottom right of every page, that lets you:
    • change the text size
    • switch to dark or light high contrast, or grayscale
    • highlight links and headings
    • switch to a readable font or wider text spacing
    • stop animations and videos
    • use a large cursor
    • highlight keyboard focus
    • hide images
    Your choices are remembered on your device.

Known limitations

We continue to work on improving accessibility. Despite our efforts, some parts of the site may not yet be fully accessible. For example, content from third parties, embedded external content or older documents may not be accessible. If you find a part of the site that is not accessible, please let us know so we can fix it.
