Our Expertise

With over 25 years of experience, we provide comprehensive legal services to domestic and international clients across a wide spectrum of practice areas. The firm leads high-complexity and cross-border transactions, investment deals, large-scale infrastructure and real-estate projects, and public tenders. We represent clients in commercial litigation before all courts and tribunals, including the Supreme Court. We advise on employment law, employment agreements, and labor-related disputes. The firm has extensive expertise in immigration law, including obtaining B-1 expert work visas for foreign professionals. In addition, we offer full estate planning services, including wills, succession matters, and tailored personal legal solutions.

Who we are

Dardik, Gross & Co. is a leading boutique law firm based in the Tel Aviv District, specializing in a broad range of commercial and civil law matters. The firm is well-regarded for handling complex legal issues, guiding sophisticated transactions, and supporting major projects for both Israeli and international companies. Our deep understanding of the Israeli business environment, combined with robust international capabilities, enables us to deliver precise, effective, and solution-oriented legal advice. The firm is consistently ranked by top legal publications and rating companies as one of Israel's leading law firms, recognized for professionalism, responsiveness, and a high standard of client service.

DE&I Commitment
