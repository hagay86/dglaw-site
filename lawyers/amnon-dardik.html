Adv. Amnon Dardik

Founder & Managing Partner, Head of Civil & Commercial Law

dardik@dglaw.co.il

Advocate Amnon Dardik is a founding partner at Dardik, Gross & Co., heads the civil-commercial department and coordinates the firm’s litigation activity.

Amnon commands intricate knowledge of Israeli law and business-making, gained through years of representing his clients’ interests in all major fields of industry: marketing and distribution, franchise, contractor work and other real estate matters, the automobile industry, security and cleaning, pharmaceuticals, heavy industry, electronics, financial markets, public bodies, political parties and non-profits, and kibbutz industries.

Amnon is the Israeli delegate to ALFA – a prestigious global legal network.

Amnon is active in non-profit organizations, tending to public goods such as education, sports and protection of the environment.
