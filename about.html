About Us

Dardik, Gross & Co. is a boutique law firm that provides comprehensive and complete legal services to its clients. Located in the heart of the Israeli business district, we specialize in commercial and civil law. The firm enjoys an excellent reputation for its rich experience in handling legal and business matters, both local and international.

Dardik, Gross & Co. is ranked by many prestigious publications (both domestic and international) as one of the leading firms in Israel in international commercial law, immigration law (including handling work visas to Israel and visas abroad), mergers and acquisitions, and franchise law. Among other accolades, Dardik, Gross & Co. has been consistently ranked as a leading firm in international commercial law by both Dun & Bradstreet and BDI guides for the years 2011–2026. BDI has awarded the firm two separate prizes: one as a leading firm in international commercial law, and one for our immigration department. In the years 2018–2023, our firm was ranked by BDI as an “Elite” firm in this field.

Our firm is characterized by extensive international activity based on our full mastery of foreign languages and nearly two decades of experience in supporting multinational corporations. We provide our clients with comprehensive and ongoing legal service in a One-Stop-Shop style, ensuring that each client receives the highest level of legal service tailored precisely to their needs.

Our firm supports companies and individuals, both Israeli and foreign, and provides them with the cutting-edge legal services they need – in every aspect of their business activities. Each client receives a service package tailored to their unique needs. Our firm supports Israeli clients in their overseas business activities, thanks to our deep business understanding and our proficiency in English and Spanish. This activity, in turn, has endowed our firm with extensive knowledge and experience in international transactions.

Our firm is the exclusive Israeli representative of ALFA International, a legal network comprising 150 independent law firms worldwide.
